# Heroku Usage Tracker - One-Page Executive Brief

---

## 🎯 What It Is
A centralized monitoring platform that tracks Heroku resource usage across multiple accounts and sends automated alerts before exceeding quotas, preventing unexpected costs.

## 💼 Business Problem
- ❌ No visibility into Heroku resource consumption
- ❌ Surprise overage charges
- ❌ Manual monitoring across multiple accounts is time-consuming
- ❌ Reactive cost management

---

## ✅ Solution Delivered
**Real-time monitoring dashboard** with automated email alerts for:
- Dyno hours consumption
- Heroku Connect usage  
- Add-on costs
- Multi-account support (Personal, Team, Enterprise)
